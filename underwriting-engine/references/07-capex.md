# 07: Capital Expenditure Planning

## Hold Horizon Context

- **EFB deals**: fixed 10-year hold matching bond maturity. Capex must maintain bond covenant compliance, AMI-tenant satisfaction, and ROFR exercise value at Year 10.
- **ACQ value-add**: typical 3–7 year hold. Capex front-loaded in Year 1–2 to drive rent gains.
- **ACQ core-plus**: 7–10 year hold. Minimal capex unless deferred maintenance is identified.

---

## Renovation Scope Classification

| Scope | Cost/Unit | Hurdle Premium | Typical Work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | <$5K | +0 bps | Paint, fixtures, hardware |
| Moderate | $5–15K | +0 bps | Flooring, appliances, countertops, fixtures |
| Heavy | $15–30K | Per vintage tier | Full interior gut, kitchen/bath remodel |
| Luxury/Full | $30K+ | Per vintage tier | Down-to-studs, systems replacement |

## Four-Bucket Capex Framework

### Bucket 1: Interior Renovation
- Cost per unit based on scope (light/moderate/heavy)
- Differentiate renovated vs. unrenovated units
- Unrenovated: Full scope cost
- Previously renovated (SLV/silver): Touch-up cost (typically 25–35% of full scope)

### Bucket 2: Exterior & Common Areas
- Target: 15–20% of interior renovation budget (QC check)
- Includes: Façade, signage, clubhouse, pool, fitness, landscaping, parking
- Amenity upgrades that drive rent premiums

### Bucket 3: Deferred Maintenance
- Sourced from PCA (Property Condition Assessment)
- Categories: Immediate (0–12 months), Near-term (1–3 years), Mid-term (3–5 years)
- Common items: HVAC, water heaters, plumbing, roof, electrical panels
- Budget separately from renovation, these don't drive rent premiums

### Bucket 4: Soft Costs
- Construction management fee: 3–5% of hard costs
- Contingency: See vintage-based table below
- Permits and fees: $15–30K typical
- Architecture/design: $5–15K if needed

## Contingency Budget by Property Age

| Property Age | Contingency % | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| <20 years | 8–10% | Modern construction, predictable scope |
| 20–30 years | 10–12% | Aging systems, some hidden conditions |
| 30–40 years | 12–15% | Major systems, functional updates |
| 40+ years | 15%+ | High unforeseen risk, asbestos/lead potential |

## Budget Validation

### Contractor Bidding
- Minimum 3 bids for projects >$500K
- Bids should be within 15% of each other
- If one bid is >25% below others, investigate scope gaps

### Per-Unit Cost Reasonableness
| Market | Light Reno | Moderate | Heavy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gateway | $5–8K | $12–20K | $25–45K |
| Secondary | $3–6K | $8–15K | $18–35K |
| Tertiary | $2–5K | $6–12K | $15–25K |

## ROI Analysis

### Renovation ROI Calculation
```
Monthly Rent Premium = Post-Reno Rent - Pre-Reno Rent
Annual Premium = Monthly Premium × 12
Renovation ROI = Annual Premium ÷ Renovation Cost per Unit
```

**Minimum threshold: 8% cash-on-cash ROI on renovation spend.**

### ROI by Scope
| Scope | Typical ROI Range | Minimum Acceptable |
|---|---|---|
| Light (<$5K) | 30–60% | 8% |
| Moderate ($5–15K) | 15–35% | 8% |
| Heavy ($15–30K) | 8–20% | 8% |
| Luxury ($30K+) | 5–15% | 8% |

If ROI is below 8%, the renovation doesn't justify the capital outlay.

## Timeline Planning

### Standard Renovation Timelines
| Scope | Per-Unit Duration | Full Property (100+ units) |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 3–5 days | 6–12 months |
| Moderate | 5–10 days | 12–18 months |
| Heavy | 10–20 days | 18–24 months |

### Phasing Strategy
- Renovate 8–15 units/month for properties >100 units
- Maintain occupancy above 80% during renovation
- Prioritize vacant units first, then non-renewals
- Batch by building to minimize disruption

## QC Checklist
- [ ] Interior scope costed per unit type (reno vs. unreno)
- [ ] Exterior budget is 15–20% of interior
- [ ] Deferred maintenance identified separately from value-add capex
- [ ] Contingency at vintage-appropriate percentage
- [ ] Construction management fee included (3–5%)
- [ ] Renovation ROI exceeds 8% threshold
- [ ] Timeline realistic for unit count and scope
- [ ] At least 3 contractor bids obtained (for projects >$500K)
