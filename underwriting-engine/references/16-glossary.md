# 16: Glossary (EFB, ACQ, and General CRE)

## EFB-Specific Terms

- **AMI (Area Median Income):** HUD-published income level for an MSA. Rent limits set as % of AMI (e.g., 80% AMI). Updated annually.
- **B-Piece:** Subordinate debt held by Project Administrator (Shieldstone). Typically 5% of project cost when developer fee is split 5%/5%. Pari passu maturity with A-piece bonds.
- **Bond Trustee:** Independent fiduciary managing bond proceeds and enforcing covenants.
- **Developer Fee:** Primary GP compensation in EFB deals. Standard: 10% of total project cost. May be split 5% cash / 5% B-piece.
- **EFB (Essential Function Bonds):** Tax-exempt bonds issued by qualified non-profit/quasi-governmental entity for workforce housing. (Sometimes called "Exempt Facility Bonds" but the firm uses "Essential Function Bonds.")
- **FMR (Fair Market Rent):** HUD-published rent levels by MSA and bedroom count. Basis for HAP voucher payments.
- **HAP (Housing Assistance Payment):** Section 8 voucher payment. Government-guaranteed rental income.
- **HFA (Housing Finance Authority):** State or local quasi-governmental entity that issues tax-exempt bonds.
- **HFC (Housing Finance Corporation):** Texas Chapter 394 vehicle for property tax exemption.
- **Issuer:** Entity that issues the tax-exempt bonds. Must be qualified non-profit or quasi-governmental.
- **Novogradac:** Publisher of LIHTC rent and income limits by county and AMI tier. Primary source for AMI rent ceilings via novoco.com (manual paste, anti-bot blocks scraping).
- **PFC (Public Facility Corporation):** Texas Chapter 303 vehicle for property tax exemption.
- **PILOT (Payment in Lieu of Taxes):** Used in GA bond-lease structures. 40–60% of fee-simple ad valorem, NOT $0.
- **Project Administrator:** Shieldstone's role in EFB transactions. Manages project without owning property.
- **QMC (Qualified Management Contract):** IRS safe harbor (Rev. Proc. 2017-13) governing project administrator compensation.
- **ROFR (Right of First Refusal):** Contractual right for Shieldstone to acquire property at bond maturity (Year 10).
- **SAFMR (Small Area Fair Market Rent):** ZIP-code-level FMR for designated metros (DFW, Houston, Atlanta, etc.). Critical for HAP tier sizing, differs materially from county-level FMR.
- **Tax Exemption:** Full property tax elimination via non-profit/governmental ownership. Structural to EFB economics.
- **Turbo Amortization:** Principal payment only from cash available after opex and interest. No default if no cash for principal.

## ACQ-Specific Terms

- **90/90 Rule:** Property must achieve >=90% economic occupancy for 90 consecutive days before agency refinance closes. Refi gate for traditional value-add; NOT closing gate for near-stabilized core-plus.
- **Agency Refi:** Refinance into Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac multifamily debt at 1.25x DSCR, 75% LTV (up to 80%), 30-year amort.
- **Bridge-to-Agency:** Standard value-add capital structure: floating-rate bridge during reno/lease-up, refi into agency at stabilization.
- **Bridge-to-HUD:** Alternative pattern using HUD 223(f) takeout instead of agency. Better LTV for affordable.
- **Core-Plus:** Near-stabilized, institutional-quality, light value-add. Sits between Core (stabilized) and Value-Add (renovation-heavy).
- **HUD 223(f):** Federal Housing Administration's multifamily refinance program. LTV up to 87% for affordable.
- **HUD MAP Guide:** Handbook 4430.G. Bible for HUD multifamily underwriting standards.
- **Live Local Act:** Florida §196.1979. For-profit ownership with annual recertification. NOT lender-underwritable as primary; upside layer only.
- **MAP (Multifamily Accelerated Processing):** HUD's expedited processing for multifamily insured loans.
- **Reassessment Ratio:** % of purchase price at which county reassesses upon sale. FL 65–80%, TX 60–70%, GA 40%.
- **T-3 Annualized:** Trailing 3-month NOI × 4. Agency sizing basis.

## General CRE Terms

- **Bad Debt:** Uncollected rent expense. Stripped from EGI.
- **Box Score:** Monthly performance summary from PM software (Yardi, RealPage). Shows month-by-month occupancy.
- **BTCF:** Before-Tax Cash Flow = NOI - Debt Service.
- **Cap Rate:** NOI ÷ Property Value. Going-in = NOI ÷ PP. Exit = Projected NOI ÷ Sale Price.
- **CHDO (Community Housing Development Organization):** State-certified nonprofit for Texas §11.1825 exemption.
- **CoC (Cash-on-Cash):** Annual BTCF ÷ Total Equity.
- **CoStar:** Commercial real estate data provider. Source for rent comps, sales comps, submarket vacancy, pipeline.
- **DSCR:** NOI ÷ Annual Debt Service. EFB minimum: 1.15x. Agency minimum: 1.25x. HUD 223(f) market-rate: 1.176x.
- **DUS Guide:** Fannie Mae Delegated Underwriting and Servicing Guide. Lender underwriting standards.
- **EGI:** Effective Gross Income = GPR - Vacancy + Other Income + Utility Reimbursements.
- **EM (Equity Multiple):** Total Distributions ÷ Total Equity. Shieldstone minimum: 1.5x for 5-year hold.
- **GP (General Partner):** Sponsor party. Receives promote.
- **GP Equity:** Sponsor co-invest. Typically 5–10% of total equity.
- **GPR (Gross Potential Rent):** Total rent if 100% occupied at underwritten rents.
- **Gateway / Secondary / Tertiary:** Shieldstone market tier classification by MSA population.
- **IRR:** Internal Rate of Return. Rate making NPV of all cash flows = 0. Shieldstone absolute minimum: 14% levered, 15% net to investor.
- **I/O (Interest-Only):** No principal payments during the period. EFB bonds: full-term I/O. Bridge loans typically 1–3 year IO.
- **LIHTC (Low-Income Housing Tax Credit):** 4% or 9% federal tax credit for affordable housing. NOT used in EFB structures (EFB is governmental purpose, not LIHTC).
- **LP (Limited Partner):** Capital partner. Receives pref + share of distributions per waterfall.
- **LTV:** Loan-to-Value = Loan ÷ Property Value. EFB target: bonds = 100%+ of TPC. Agency standard: 75% (up to 80%).
- **LURA (Land Use Restriction Agreement):** Recorded affordability covenant tied to FL §196.1978 or LIHTC.
- **MIP (Mortgage Insurance Premium):** HUD-insured loan ongoing fee. Adds 25–60 bps to effective rate.
- **MLA:** Market-rate Locally Affordable. 4-tier mixed-income rent structure component. Capped at FMR.
- **MUD (Municipal Utility District):** Texas special district. Assessments NOT covered by governmental ownership exemption.
- **NOI:** Net Operating Income = EGI - Total OpEx.
- **OM (Offering Memorandum):** Broker's marketing document for a property sale.
- **PCA (Property Condition Assessment):** Engineering report identifying deferred maintenance and capex needs.
- **PID (Public Improvement District):** Texas special district funding infrastructure. Assessments continue regardless of ownership.
- **Promote:** GP performance fee above pref hurdle. Standard Shieldstone: 8% pref → 70/30 to 15% IRR → 50/50 above.
- **RUBS (Ratio Utility Billing System):** Recovery: 60–80% of owner-paid utilities. Default 75%.
- **T-12:** Trailing twelve months operating statement.
- **TPC (Total Project Cost):** PP + Closing + Capex + Reserves + Dev Fee + Bond COI (EFB).
- **YOC (Yield on Cost):** Stabilized NOI ÷ Total Project Cost.
