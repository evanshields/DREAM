## The idea

DREAM is the operating system for clear real-estate decisions: one place to move from first look to an evidence-backed investment decision. It combines the sales book, the underwriting brain, and shared memory without pretending that AI is the source of truth.

**Positioning:** The operating system for clear real-estate decisions.
**Promise:** Every deal. One clear path to a decision.
**Supporting line:** From first look to investment decision.

## Personality

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Calmly intelligent | Flashy or theatrical |
| Evidence-led | “AI magic” |
| Institutional but approachable | Cold or bureaucratic |
| Decisive | Reckless or overconfident |
| Built by operators | Generic software for everyone |

The voice is warm, direct, and concise. DREAM names what is known, what is missing, what is blocked, and what happens next. It shows evidence instead of performing intelligence.

## Relationship to Shieldstone

DREAM should share Shieldstone's restraint, warmth, and institutional credibility—not copy its identity. Shared family cues are Deep Teal, Dark Slate, Off-White, thin rules, disciplined spacing, and selective editorial typography. DREAM earns distinction through its own mark, a compact product-first interface, and an Iris signal color.

Use **DREAM — by Shieldstone** while trust and distribution are closely tied to the parent brand. The DREAM name should remain visually dominant.

## Visual language

- **Core:** Deep Teal `#005253`, Dark Slate `#3C4856`, Ink `#171C26`, Off-White `#FAFAF8`.
- **DREAM signal:** Iris `#6558E8`, used for agent activity, conversation, drafts, and active focus.
- **Warm structure:** Taupe `#D4C4B0`, used for dividers and human-review surfaces.
- **Meaning is fixed:** Teal = engine-confirmed; Iris = agent-generated or proposed; Amber = human review required; Red = blocked or failed; Green = completed or passed. Never use Iris to imply a deterministic result.
- **Meaning is never color-only:** Every state also needs a plain-English label and, where useful, an icon. New semantic tokens must be accessibility-tested before existing CRM colors are migrated.
- **Typography:** Noto Sans or Instrument Sans for the product; Josefin Sans or a compact sans for labels; Playfair Display or Instrument Serif only for landing-page and major editorial moments.
- **Motion:** Quiet transitions, progress, and state change. Nothing loops, bounces, or glows for decoration.

## Mark concept

Build the DREAM symbol as a **deconstruction of Shieldstone's rounded interlocking mark**, reassembled into a “Convergent D” formed by three paths: CRM, agentic infrastructure, and shared memory joining into one decision. The family relationship should be recognizable without duplicating the Shieldstone symbol. It must remain legible at favicon size and work in one color. Avoid shields, houses, rooflines, brains, sparkles, robots, and ornamental AI imagery.

The original vendor's deconstructed Shieldstone study should be treated as a reference candidate when it is located—not assumed to be the final mark. Compare its geometry, ownership rights, small-size legibility, and one-color performance directly against the Convergent D before finalizing the logo.

## Product principles

1. The interface should feel like a calm deal room, not a chatbot wrapped around a spreadsheet.
2. Numbers and gates look authoritative only when verified by deterministic systems.
3. Human-review moments are visible and dignified, never treated as friction.
4. Empty, stale, disputed, and blocked states must be visually honest.
5. Dense information gets hierarchy and breathing room—not decorative cards everywhere.

## Direction decision

Historical exploration only: the three original directions are preserved in `DREAM_BRAND_DIRECTIONS_V0.1.png`.

1. **Quiet Signal — recommended:** the Convergent D uses deconstructed family-mark DNA; balanced, distinctive, and easiest to extend across CRM and landing page.
2. **Decision Grid:** most conservative, analytical, and institutional.
3. **Open Current:** most contemporary, expressive, and agent-forward.

After selection, produce the final mini kit: primary/compact/reversed marks, favicon, color tokens, typography rules, light/dark application examples, voice rules, and a CRM implementation checklist.

## Open decisions

- **Recommended now:** Keep DREAM in all caps; do not force an acronym expansion.
- **Needs selection:** Quiet Signal, Decision Grid, or Open Current.
